# Section A — Integrated Marketing Plan

## Inputs
Business type + core offer, target audience + ICP, current channels + performance data, monthly budget, team size, primary growth goal, timeline.

## Deliverables

### Channel Strategy
Rank channels by expected ROI for this business. Allocate budget % with rationale. Define primary vs. support channels and interdependencies.

### AI Tool Stack
Research and recommend tools per function (content creation, email, social, SEO, CRM, ads) with pricing and integration notes.

### Channel Playbooks (5)
**Content**: Pillars, formats, AI-assisted creation workflow, repurposing system (1 piece → 8 formats), cadence
**Email**: Segmentation strategy, behavioral triggers, broadcast calendar, A/B testing framework
**Paid Ads**: Platform recommendations + budget split, campaign structure, creative rotation, performance alert triggers
**Social**: Platform priority, content mix ratios, scheduling automation, DM workflow
**SEO**: Keyword framework, content gap process, internal linking, rank tracking alerts

### 60 Content Ideas (10 per format)
Blog / Email subjects / Social hooks / Video concepts / Ad angles / Lead magnets
Each includes: the AI prompt to generate the full piece.

### 3 Core Email Sequences (copy-paste ready)
Welcome (5 emails) / Lead nurture (7 emails) / Re-engagement (3 emails)

### Budget Allocation Model (Sheets)
| Channel | % of Budget | Monthly $ | Expected CPL | Expected Volume | Notes |

### 90-Day Timeline
Month 1: Foundation (tools, tracking, first assets) → Month 2: Full activation → Month 3: Optimize and scale

# Section B — Funnel Design

## Inputs
Primary offer, customer acquisition method, AOV/LTV, traffic sources, tech stack preference, funnel goal.

## Core Psychology Framework Applied to Every Funnel
AIDA structure + objection sequencing + risk reversal + commitment escalation + urgency architecture.

## 5 Funnel Templates

**Opt-In Funnel**: Landing page structure, thank you page, lead magnet delivery email, 3-email post-opt-in nurture

**Webinar Funnel**: Registration page, confirmation + calendar copy, 4-email reminder sequence (7d/1d/1hr/during), show-up optimization tactics, post-webinar follow-up (attended/no-show/replay)

**VSL Funnel**: Pre-headline + page structure, VSL script structure (hook/story/pivot/offer/CTA), below-fold support copy, order page copy, upsell/downsell page

**Free Trial Funnel**: Signup page, onboarding sequence (Day 0/1/3/5/7/end), conversion trigger email, expiry sequence

**Product Launch Funnel**: 3-piece pre-launch sequence, launch day email, 3-day launch sequence, close cart email, waitlist capture

## Also Deliver
- Copywriting frameworks per funnel stage
- Conversion rate benchmarks (by industry and funnel type)
- Tool comparison: ConvertKit / GoHighLevel / Unbounce / ClickFunnels
- Revenue projection model (Sheets): Traffic | Opt-in % | Leads | Sales % | Conversions | AOV | Revenue | ROAS

# Section C — Agency Positioning & Service Packaging

## Inputs
Agency focus area, automation platforms used, current services + pricing, ideal client profile, team size + capacity, revenue goal.

## Deliverables

### 8 Tiered Service Packages
From Starter Automation (~$500–1,500/mo) through full Retention + Expansion System. Per package: name + positioning hook, exact deliverables, platforms required, implementation timeline, retainer component, ideal client profile.

### Owner Psychology Audit (if targeting small business/owner-operators)
Map: primary fear, primary desire, skepticism triggers, trust builders, decision process. Design entry product (low risk, fast win), proof-of-value milestone, natural expansion path.

### Sales System
- Discovery call framework (20-min structured, not 60-min)
- One-page visual proposal template
- 3-email post-proposal follow-up sequence
- Objection scripts for owner-specific resistance: burned before / no budget / need to think / guarantee? / can do it myself

### 20 ROI Case Studies
100–150 words each. Client type + problem + solution + specific results (%, $, hours saved). Written in buyer's language.

### Pricing Architecture
Project vs. retainer vs. performance hybrid analysis. Upsell/cross-sell paths. Scope boundary definitions. Discount strategy.

### 90-Day Launch Roadmap
Month 1: Positioning + case studies + first 10 outreach conversations
Month 2: Content live + 3–5 clients onboarded + referral network seeded
Month 3: Results documented + referral system live + paid acquisition test

### Financial Projections
| Quarter | Clients | Avg MRR | Revenue | COGS | Profit | Margin |
Year 1 + Year 3 views. Includes CAC, churn assumptions, expansion revenue.
